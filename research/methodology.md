
# Investment Research Methodology

## 1. Research Process

The investment process follows five stages:

1. Define the eligible security universe.
2. Evaluate fundamentals and valuation.
3. Rank securities using quantitative factors.
4. Construct a risk-aware portfolio.
5. Monitor performance and rebalance.

## 2. Fundamental Research

Each company will be evaluated using:

### Financial Strength
- Debt levels and interest coverage
- Free cash flow generation
- Liquidity and operating stability

### Business Quality
- Return on invested capital
- Profit margins
- Competitive positioning

### Growth
- Revenue growth
- Earnings growth
- Free cash flow growth

### Valuation
- Discounted cash flow analysis
- Relative valuation multiples
- Scenario-based intrinsic value estimates

## 3. Quantitative Screening

Four initial categories:

| Factor | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Valuation | 30% |
| Quality | 30% |
| Growth | 20% |
| Momentum | 20% |

Each metric will be normalized across
the eligible investment universe.

Metric directions, missing-value treatment,
and sector comparability rules will be
specified before backtesting.

## 4. Portfolio Optimization

Portfolio weights will be determined using
expected returns, estimated covariance,
and portfolio constraints.

Multiple allocation methods will be
compared against simpler baselines.

## 5. Validation

Models will be evaluated using historical
out-of-sample testing where feasible.

Data timestamps and rebalance assumptions
will be documented.

## 6. Research Outputs

Each investment thesis will include:

- Company overview
- Investment rationale
- Financial analysis
- Valuation assumptions
- Risks and potential catalysts
- Portfolio allocation rationale
